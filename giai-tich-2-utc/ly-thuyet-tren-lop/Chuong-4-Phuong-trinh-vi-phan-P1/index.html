Hình 6: Bài tập mẫu Cauchy

$$y' - \frac{4}{x+1}y = 2(x+1)$$

$$\Rightarrow y = \left( C + \int 2(x+1) \cdot e^{\int -\frac{4}{x+1}dx} dx \right) \cdot e^{\int \frac{4}{x+1}dx}$$

$$= \left( C + \int 2(x+1) \cdot e^{-4\ln|x+1|} dx \right) \cdot e^{4\ln|x+1|}$$

$$= \left( C + \int \frac{2 \cdot (x+1)}{(x+1)^4} dx \right) \cdot (x+1)^4$$

$$= \left( C + 2 \frac{(x+1)^{-2}}{-2} \right) (x+1)^4$$

$$= \left( C - (x+1)^{-2} \right) \cdot (x+1)^4$$

$$= C \cdot (x+1)^4 - (x+1)^2$$


Mà $y(0) = 2 \Rightarrow C - 1 = 2 \Rightarrow C = 3$

$$\Rightarrow y = 3(x+1)^4 - (x+1)^2$$
